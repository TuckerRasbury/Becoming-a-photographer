# Workflow 02 — Pre-Production

*Track B clients only. This is what triggers the producer fee (50% of day rate).*

*Covers: creative brief → location scout → shot list → day-of logistics*

---

## When this workflow applies

Pre-production is required — and the producer fee is charged — whenever Isaac performs documented pre-production work for a Track B client. "Documented" means at least one of the following:

- A recorded briefing call
- A written creative brief or shot list
- A location scout (in-person or remote)
- A logistics or timeline plan

If none of this is needed, the producer fee does not apply.

---

## Step 1: Creative brief call

Schedule a recorded briefing call with the client. Clients consent to recording as a condition of the process.

Cover on the call:
- [ ] What story are we telling?
- [ ] Who are the key people / subjects?
- [ ] What moments / shots are non-negotiable (must-haves)?
- [ ] What is the intended use of the images? (Confirm licensing scope from intake)
- [ ] Any restrictions — people who don't want to be photographed, areas off-limits, etc.?
- [ ] Timeline for the day
- [ ] Delivery format preferences (full gallery? selects only?)

Document the call in writing after. This is the project brief.

---

## Step 2: Location scout

For shoots at unfamiliar locations:
- [ ] Visit or research the location
- [ ] Identify best light at the shoot time (golden hour? indoor light sources?)
- [ ] Note any permit requirements — **client is responsible for all location permits** (Q11 — clause to be drafted)
- [ ] Identify backup plan if weather or access changes

---

## Step 3: Shot list

Build a shot list based on the brief. Organize by:
1. Must-haves (non-negotiable)
2. Should-haves (strong additions if time allows)
3. Nice-to-haves (opportunistic — if the story opens up)

Share the shot list with the client for confirmation before the shoot.

---

## Step 4: Day-of logistics plan

- [ ] Confirm call time and location with client
- [ ] Confirm timeline (arrival, shoot window, wrap)
- [ ] Confirm payment schedule — when is remaining balance due? (Q12 — OPEN)
- [ ] Pack equipment checklist (see Workflow 03)

Move to **Workflow 03 — Shoot Day**.
