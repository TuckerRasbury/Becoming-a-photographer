# Workflow 03 — Shoot Day

*Covers: equipment check → arrival → shooting → wrap*

---

## Equipment checklist

Pack before every shoot:

**Camera & lenses**
- [ ] Canon EOS Rebel SL3 body
- [ ] 50mm lens
- [ ] 55–150mm zoom lens
- [ ] Fully charged battery (+ spare if available)
- [ ] Formatted memory cards (multiple)
- [ ] Lens cloths

**Lighting**
- [ ] Handheld light (charged)
- [ ] Tripod / camera stand (if needed for the shoot type)

**Admin**
- [ ] Shot list (Track B)
- [ ] Client contact info
- [ ] Signed contract on file (confirmed before leaving)

---

## Arrival

- Arrive 15–20 minutes early for setup and light assessment
- For events: introduce yourself to the point of contact before shooting begins
- Confirm timeline with client on arrival

---

## Shooting

- Shoot to serve the story, not to hit a photo count quota — delivered photo estimates are ranges, not targets
- Follow the shot list (Track B), but stay alert to unplanned moments
- For community / documentary work: respect public-space awareness and subject dignity
- Subjects at public events (protests, rallies) do not require releases for documentary use — but be thoughtful
- For portrait / commercial sessions: model releases may be required (Q8 — OPEN, form not yet drafted)

---

## Wrap

- [ ] Confirm all must-have shots are captured before calling wrap
- [ ] Thank client / point of contact
- [ ] Communicate next steps: editing turnaround, delivery platform (Pixiset), revision process
- [ ] Remind client of the standard turnaround: **3–5 business days**
- [ ] If rush delivery was not discussed at booking, it cannot be applied retroactively

Move to **Workflow 04 — Post-Production**.
