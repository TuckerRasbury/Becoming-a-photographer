# Workflow 04 — Post-Production & Delivery

*Covers: card backup → culling → editing → delivery → revisions*

---

## Step 1: Back up immediately after the shoot

Before anything else:
- [ ] Copy all RAW files to primary storage
- [ ] Copy all RAW files to backup storage (separate drive or cloud)
- [ ] Verify backup before formatting memory cards

**RAW files are never delivered to clients under any circumstances.** They stay with Isaac.

---

## Step 2: Cull

In Lightroom: go through all frames and flag selects.

Aim for:
- Narrative coverage (beginning, middle, end of the event or session)
- Technical quality (sharp focus, good exposure)
- Emotional resonance — shoot to serve the story

Photo count targets are **estimates, not quotas**:
- 2-hour shoot: 25–75 edited photos
- 4-hour event: 50–150 edited photos
- Full day: 100–200+ edited photos
- Full-day wedding: 200–300+ edited photos

---

## Step 3: Edit

In Lightroom (and Photoshop for detail work):
- Color correction and grading
- Exposure adjustments
- Retouching (portraits)
- Export to delivery format for Pixiset

Isaac retains **creative discretion** — the edit reflects his artistic intent. Clients can request revisions (see Step 5), but Isaac may decline requests that conflict with the integrity of the work.

---

## Step 4: Deliver via Pixiset

- [ ] Create gallery in Pixiset
- [ ] Upload final edited files
- [ ] Configure download permissions per the contract (what the client licensed)
- [ ] Send gallery link to client with a note on the revision process
- [ ] Confirm standard turnaround was met (3–5 business days from shoot)

If rush delivery was booked:
- Rush (3 business days): surcharge already applied — confirm delivery
- Super rush (2 business days): surcharge already applied — confirm delivery

---

## Step 5: Revisions

**2 revision rounds are included in the $400 post-production fee.**

Each revision round:
1. Schedule a **recorded call** with the client — they consent to recording as a condition of the process
2. Collect all feedback on the call (do not accept feedback by text/email without a call)
3. Edit based on the documented feedback
4. Re-deliver within 3–5 business days

Additional rounds: **$100 per round**, same turnaround.

Isaac may exercise **creative discretion** and decline revision requests that conflict with the artistic intent or narrative integrity of the work.

---

## Step 6: Close the project

- [ ] Final payment received (per payment terms — Q12 OPEN)
- [ ] Confirm client has downloaded their gallery
- [ ] Archive RAW files to long-term storage
- [ ] Add selects to portfolio pipeline (subject to model release if applicable)
- [ ] Note any testimonial / referral opportunity
