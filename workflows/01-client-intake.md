# Workflow 01 — Client Intake

*Covers: first contact → qualification → proposal → booking confirmation*

---

## Step 1: Receive inquiry

Inquiries come through the portfolio site contact form (or direct referral). Respond within 24 hours.

Gather the following on first contact or via the pre-shoot questionnaire (Q14 — OPEN):

- [ ] Event type (portrait, event, commercial, documentary)
- [ ] Date, time, and location
- [ ] Approximate duration
- [ ] Intended use of images (personal? social? website? print? advertising?)
- [ ] Budget range (opens the community rate / scope conversation if needed)
- [ ] How they found you

---

## Step 2: Qualify the project

Determine which pricing track applies before building a proposal.

| If the client is... | Use... |
|--------------------|--------|
| Individual, family, personal connection, celebration | **Track A** — hourly rate + kit + post-production |
| Organization, gym, business, nonprofit, event with commercial use | **Track B** — day rate + producer fee (if pre-production) + kit + post-production + licensing |

If the client mentions budget constraints and the work fits your civic/community focus, consider offering the **community rate** — fewer hours, fewer deliverables, limited licensing. Frame it as a scope adjustment: *"Tell me your budget and we'll find a scope that works."*

---

## Step 3: Build the proposal

Using the numbers from `constitution/business-constitution.md`:

**Track A proposal line items:**
1. On-site photography (hours × $200)
2. Kit fee ($75–$100)
3. Editing & delivery ($400 flat)
4. Rush delivery surcharge (if applicable — offer upfront, not retroactively)
5. Deposit options (25% → 5% off, 50% → 10% off creative fee)

**Track B proposal line items:**
1. Creative fee (hours × $200 or day rate)
2. Producer fee (50% of day rate, if pre-production is documented)
3. Kit fee ($75–$100)
4. Editing & delivery ($400 flat)
5. Licensing (run through the 5 licensing variables — see Section 3.2 of constitution)
6. Rush delivery surcharge (if applicable)
7. Deposit options

Estimated delivered photos are **estimates only** — state them as ranges, not guarantees.

---

## Step 4: Send proposal and contract

Contract must express all principles from Section 4.1 of the constitution before being sent. The following clauses are still OPEN and need to be drafted before taking a paid commercial client (see Q6–Q13 in open-questions.md):

- Cancellation fee structure
- Rescheduling limits
- Model / subject release
- Payment terms
- Late payment clause
- Permit responsibility

---

## Step 5: Confirm booking

Booking is confirmed when:

- [ ] Signed contract received
- [ ] Deposit received (if applicable)
- [ ] Date locked on calendar
- [ ] Pre-shoot questionnaire sent (Track B: creative brief initiated)

For Track B clients, move to **Workflow 02 — Pre-Production**.
For Track A clients, move directly to **Workflow 03 — Shoot Day**.
