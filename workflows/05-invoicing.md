# Workflow 05 — Invoicing & Pricing

*A quick reference for building accurate proposals. All numbers from the constitution.*

---

## Track A — Deliverable-based (families, portraits, personal, celebrations)

| Line item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Hourly rate | $200/hr |
| 2-hour minimum | $400 |
| Half-day (4 hrs) | $800 |
| Full day (8 hrs) | $1,600 |
| Kit fee | $75–$100 |
| Editing & delivery | $400 flat |
| Rush (3 business days) | + $100 (total $500) |
| Super rush (2 business days) | + $200 (total $600) |

**Deposit discounts (applied to creative fee only):**
- No deposit: full price
- 25% deposit upfront: 5% off creative fee
- 50% deposit upfront: 10% off creative fee
- All deposits are nonrefundable

---

## Track B — Day-rate / project-scope (orgs, gyms, events, commercial, nonprofits)

| Line item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Creative fee (day rate, 8 hrs) | $1,600 |
| Creative fee (half-day, 4 hrs) | $800 |
| Producer fee (pre-production documented) | 50% of applicable day rate |
| Kit fee | $75–$100 |
| Editing & delivery | $400 flat |
| Licensing | Additive — run through 5 variables below |
| Rush surcharges | Same as Track A |

**Licensing — the 5 variables to ask:**
1. Medium (social? website? print? broadcast? paid ads?)
2. Reach / audience size
3. Duration (6 mo? 1 year? 2 years?)
4. Exclusivity (non-exclusive is default — exclusive costs significantly more)
5. Geography (SoCal only? US? International?)

**Licensing benchmarks (Southern California, non-exclusive):**

| Use case | Range |
|----------|-------|
| Community org, social only, 6–12 months | $150–$350 (often included in base) |
| Small business, website + social, 1 year | $300–$700 |
| Small business, website + social + print, 1 year | $600–$1,200 |
| Org, multi-channel, 1–2 years | $1,000–$2,500 |
| Commercial, paid digital advertising | $1,500–$4,000+ |
| Broadcast / TV | $3,000–$10,000+ |
| Full buyout | ~3× expanded rate |

## Quick math examples

**Track A — 2-hour portrait session, no rush, 50% deposit:**
- Creative fee: $400
- Deposit discount (10% off $400): -$40
- Kit fee: $100
- Editing: $400
- **Total: $860**

**Track A — 4-hour event, no deposit:**
- Creative fee: $800
- Kit fee: $100
- Editing: $400
- **Total: $1,300**

**Track B — Full day commercial, pre-production, Tier 2 licensing (~$500), no deposit:**
- Creative fee: $1,600
- Producer fee: $800
- Kit fee: $100
- Editing: $400
- Licensing: $500
- **Total: $3,400**
