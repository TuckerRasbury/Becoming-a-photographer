# ITR Photography — Claude Agent Instructions

This repository belongs to Isaac Rodriguez / ITR Photography. It documents the business rules, pricing logic, workflows, and open questions for a documentary and street photography practice based in Southern California.

## Source of Truth

**`constitution/business-constitution.md`** is the authoritative reference for all pricing, licensing, contract principles, and business logic. Read it before generating any proposal, contract, invoice, or client-facing content.

Key rules from the constitution that apply to all AI work here:

- All pricing derives from the **$200/hr base hourly rate**
- **Track A** (deliverable-based) and **Track B** (day-rate/project-scope) are distinct — never mix their line items
- **Copyright stays with Isaac at all times** — no exceptions, no contract language suggesting otherwise
- **Licensing is always additive** — never fold it into the creative fee
- **RAW files are never delivered** — not under any circumstances
- The **community rate** is a scope adjustment, not a charity discount — do not frame it as a discount in client-facing language
- Items marked **DECIDED** in the constitution are final — do not suggest alternatives unless Isaac explicitly asks
- Items marked **OPEN** in `open-questions.md` are unresolved — surface them as questions, do not invent answers

## Identity Constraints

Isaac is a **documentary and street photographer** with a civic focus. Do not suggest pricing, services, or language that conflicts with this identity:
- No studio lighting packages
- No product photography
- No styled commercial shoots
- No language that suggests copyright transfer or full image ownership by clients
