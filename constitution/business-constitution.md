## 1. Definitions

These definitions apply throughout this document and in all ITR Photography contracts and proposals.

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Creative fee** | The charge for Isaac's time, expertise, creative direction, and on-site presence. This is the day rate or hourly rate. It does not include post-production, licensing, or equipment. *Example: 4-hour shoot at $200/hr = $800 creative fee.* |
| **Producer fee** | A separate charge for pre-production work performed by Isaac when acting as both photographer and producer. Covers location scouting, shot list development, client briefing calls, logistics planning, and timeline management. Charged at 50% of the day rate for any project requiring documented pre-production. *Example: Full-day shoot ($1,600 day rate) with pre-production = +$800 producer fee.* |
| **Kit fee** | A flat per-project fee covering equipment wear, depreciation, and risk. Currently $75–$100. Not a rental fee — it reflects Isaac's exposure to equipment loss or damage on a shoot. |
| **Post-production fee** | The flat fee charged for editing, retouching, color correction, and delivery of final images. Currently $400 flat regardless of shoot length. Covers 3–5 business days of turnaround. |
| **Rush delivery** | Expedited post-production turnaround requested by the client. Subject to a surcharge applied to the post-production fee. See Section 2.6 for tier definitions. |
| **Revision round** | One complete cycle of client feedback — collected on a recorded call — followed by Isaac's edits based on that feedback. Two rounds are included in the standard post-production fee. Additional rounds are $100 each. |
| **Creative discretion** | Isaac's retained right to decline revision requests that conflict with the artistic intent, narrative, or editorial integrity of the work. This is not arbitrary — it is a professional protection that ensures the work reflects Isaac's documented creative identity. |
| **Copyright** | Isaac Rodriguez / ITR Photography retains full copyright on all images at all times. Copyright never transfers to the client. Clients receive a license to use images, not ownership. |
| **License / usage rights** | A defined permission granted to a client to use specific images for specific purposes, platforms, durations, and geographies. Separate from and additive to the creative fee. All licenses are non-exclusive unless otherwise negotiated and priced accordingly. |
| **Change order** | A written request from the client to expand the scope of their license after the original agreement. Change orders trigger a new licensing fee calculation based on the expanded use. Isaac is not obligated to approve all change orders. |
| **Deposit** | A partial payment made at the time of booking to secure a date on Isaac's calendar. Currently structured as nonrefundable. Deposit size determines the discount applied to the creative fee. |
| **RAW files** | Unprocessed, unedited image files directly from the camera sensor. RAW files are never delivered to clients under any circumstances. Isaac retains all RAW files. Clients receive final edited files only. |
| **Recorded call** | A phone or video call during which client feedback is collected. Clients consent to recording as a condition of the revision process. Recording protects both parties by creating a clear record of what was requested. |
| **Retainer** | An ongoing monthly engagement where a client books a set number of shoot days per month at a bundled price. Available upon request. Not a core offering at this stage of the practice. See Section 5. |
| **Community rate** | A discounted rate extended to community clients, mutual aid organizations, or personal connections where Isaac chooses to adapt pricing to budget reality. The community rate is applied at Isaac's discretion based on defined criteria (**OPEN** — see Q16). It adjusts scope, not value. |
| **Track A** | The deliverable-based pricing track. Transparent line items: hourly rate + kit fee + post-production flat fee. Photo count estimates provided. For families, portraits, headshots, quinceañeras, celebrations. |
| **Track B** | The day-rate / project-scope pricing track. Creative fee (day rate) + producer fee (if pre-production required) + kit fee + post-production fee + licensing. For organizations, gyms, events, commercial clients, nonprofits. |

## 3. Licensing & Usage Rights

Isaac retains copyright on all images at all times. Clients receive a license — a defined permission to use images under specific conditions. Licensing is charged separately from and in addition to the creative fee. The creative fee covers Isaac's time. The licensing fee covers the value the client extracts from the images.

### 3.1 Licensing tiers — FRAMEWORK DECIDED, Tier 2 pricing OPEN

| Tier | Scope | Best for | Price |
|------|-------|----------|-------|
| Tier 1 — Limited | 1–2 platforms · 6–12 months · Non-exclusive · Local/regional | Community orgs, social media, personal use | Included in base rate |
| Tier 2 — Expanded | Multi-platform · 1–2 years · Non-exclusive · Regional/national | Small businesses, gyms, nonprofits, org websites | **OPEN** — see Q3 |
| Tier 3 — Full Buyout | All platforms · No time limit · All geographies | Rare. Priced to open a conversation, not close one. | ~3× Tier 2 rate |

### 3.2 The five licensing variables

Every licensing conversation is built around these five questions:

1. **Medium** — Social media? Website? Print? Broadcast/TV? Outdoor/billboard? Paid advertising?
2. **Reach / audience size** — Local neighborhood? Regional? National? How many followers or impressions?
3. **Duration** — 6 months? 1 year? 2 years? Open-ended?
4. **Exclusivity** — Can Isaac license the same image to other clients during the period? Exclusive rights cost significantly more.
5. **Geography** — Southern California only? US? International?

### 3.3 Licensing benchmark ranges (Southern California) — REFERENCE, not final rate card

| Use case | Suggested range | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------|
| Community org — social only, 6–12 months | $150–$350 | Included in base rate is reasonable. Upper end if significant reach. |
| Small business — website + social, 1 year | $300–$700 | Local gym, studio, restaurant. Non-exclusive. |
| Small business — website + social + print, 1 year | $600–$1,200 | Adds print (flyers, signage). More reach = higher fee. |
| Organization — multi-channel, 1–2 years | $1,000–$2,500 | Nonprofit or mid-size org with decent audience. |
| Commercial — paid digital advertising | $1,500–$4,000+ | Paid ads amplify reach significantly. |
| Broadcast / TV use | $3,000–$10,000+ | Rare at current stage. Negotiate or refer out. |
| Full buyout (all rights, indefinite) | ~3× expanded | Priced high by design to open a scope conversation. |

## 4. Contract Principles

These are the non-negotiable principles that must be expressed in all ITR Photography contracts and proposals. Specific contract language still needs to be drafted (OPEN — see open-questions.md).

### 4.1 Decided principles — DECIDED

| Principle | What it means in practice |

## 5. Retainer Policy

**AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST — NOT A CORE OFFERING**

Isaac does not currently have calendar flexibility to commit to recurring monthly engagements as a core service. Retainers are available upon request but are not marketed or prioritized at this stage of the practice.

If a retainer is requested, the following framework applies:

| Item | Structure / Notes |
|------|------------------|
| Minimum engagement | 3 months. Month-to-month not available. |
| Basic retainer (2 shoot days/month) | ~$1,200/month — bundled creative fee + kit + editing |
| Standard retainer (4 shoot days/month) | ~$2,200/month — bundled rate, slight discount vs. ad-hoc booking |
| Discount vs. ad-hoc rate | Approx. 10–15% off day rate in exchange for commitment and advance payment |
| Priority booking | Retainer clients receive calendar priority over ad-hoc bookings |
| Licensing under retainer | Images shot under a retainer are licensed for the client's standard use. Expanded or commercial use requires a separate licensing agreement. |
| Portfolio use | Isaac retains the right to use all images shot under retainer in his portfolio, unless specific images are explicitly excluded by written agreement. |

## 6. Community Pricing Philosophy

The pricing in this document reflects the real value of the work. Isaac photographs civic life, protest movements, family milestones, and community moments — not as a commercial operator, but as someone embedded in the community he shoots. That means some clients will be offered a community rate.

The community rate is not charity. It is a principled adaptation of scope — fewer hours, fewer deliverables, limited licensing — so that the work can happen without undervaluing the craft.

> "Tell me your budget and we'll find a scope that works. The work doesn't lose its value — we adjust the scope together."
